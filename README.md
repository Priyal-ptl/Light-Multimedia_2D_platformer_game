# Echoes of LIGHT

A multimedia 2D platformer that turns the many-worlds interpretation into an evolving, immersive experience.

**Play the game:** priyal-ptl.github.io/Light-Multimedia_2D_platformer_game/

## Overview

Our protagonist, a small luminous being, is an entity lost in a multiverse of worlds that continually shifts with every passing moment. In each world, they attempt to collect fragments of light and sustain their fragile existence.
